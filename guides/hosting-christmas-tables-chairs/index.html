Hosting Christmas this year? How to get the extra tables and chairs there (and back again)

Saying "come to ours this year" is the easy bit. Then you count the chairs. Most homes aren't set up for twelve round the table, so the extra seating usually comes from somewhere else: a relative's spare table, a neighbour's folding chairs, the church hall, or a second-hand find. Here's how to get it all sorted without a last-minute scramble.

1. Count the seats first

Work out how many people are coming and how many chairs you already have. Then decide what you need: an extra table, a few folding chairs, a sofa bed for anyone staying over. Measure the room so you know it will all fit with space to get round.

2. Ask early

Family, friends and community halls get asked a lot in December. Asking in November gives you the best chance, and it means you can agree when things go back.

3. Write a short list

For each item, note: - how many there are and roughly how big - whether it folds or comes apart (keep any fixings in a labelled bag) - where it's coming from, which floor, and whether there's parking nearby - the date you need it and the date it goes back

4. Getting it there

If it won't fit in the car, you can post the collection free on Pink Pumpkin Logistics (https://pinkpumpkinlogistics.co.uk), a UK site where local drivers send quotes for your job and you choose the one you're happy with. There's no obligation to accept. We move the items only, so setting the table up is down to you (and the best part).

5. Don't forget the trip back

Post the January return as a separate job at the same time, so it's one less thing to think about after the festivities. Bring everything back clean and in one piece, and you'll be asked to host again.

Have a lovely Christmas, however many chairs you end up with. 🎄
