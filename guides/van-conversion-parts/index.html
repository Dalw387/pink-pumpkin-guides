Found the perfect rock-and-roll bed for your van build, 150 miles away? Here's how to get it home

Second-hand conversion parts can save a build, but the best ones are rarely nearby, and your van is often off the road while you work on it. Here's a simple way to get the bulky bits collected.

1. Check it before you commit

Ask the seller for photos of the frame, the mounting points and any welds or wear, plus the rough size and weight. For beds and seats, ask whether it comes with the brackets and bolts, and whether it's already unbolted from their van.

2. Make sure it's ready to go

A part that's still fitted means a long wait for the driver. Ask the seller to remove it before the collection day and keep all the fixings together.

3. Pack it for the journey

Bag and label the bolts and tape them to the part they belong to. Wrap seat fabric, worktop edges and any glass or acrylic, and keep ply and insulation flat and dry. Leisure batteries and gas bottles aren't usually carried, so keep those for your own trip.

4. Describe the job clearly

Pickup and drop-off towns, each piece and its rough size, whether it needs two people, access and parking at both ends (a driveway, a unit, a storage yard), and the date you need it by.

5. Get quotes and choose

You can ring round, or post the job once and let quotes come to you. Pink Pumpkin Logistics, the site I run, lets you post it for free at pinkpumpkinlogistics.co.uk. Drivers send quotes and you pick the one you're happy with, with no obligation.

6. On the day

Have space cleared to put it down, check every piece against your list with the driver, and photograph it on arrival.

Happy building, and see you on the road in spring!
